FAQ

Quick answers before you download.

Is ClassPilot free?

Yes for up to two children. Reminders, attendance, meeting links, and fee records stay free. Upgrade to Family when you need a third child.

Do my kids need their own accounts?

No. ClassPilot is a parent and guardian tool. Children are profiles you manage. You can optionally pair a tablet to one child’s profile so they can see their schedule and join links under your control.

How do leave-by reminders work?

When you set travel minutes on a class, ClassPilot schedules an on-device reminder so you know when to leave — not only when class starts.

Does it work offline?

You can use ClassPilot on this device without cloud sign-in. Data stays on the phone. Cloud sync (optional) helps restore a family across devices and invite another adult.

Can I sync with another parent?

With a cloud account, the household owner can invite family with a one-time code. Both adults stay aligned on kids, classes, attendance, and payments after sync.

Does ClassPilot charge class tuition?

No. Recording a payment is your own fee ledger. Optional Family subscriptions are billed only through the App Store or Google Play.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete account. That removes your account and family data from the app’s cloud backend when sync is enabled.

Where is the privacy policy?

See Privacy Policy. Questions: support@classpilot.app.
